## Implemented behavior

Eligible positive OPTIDX/OPTSTK holdings with INTRADAY, CARRYFORWARD or NRML product, exact contract and lot-multiple quantity, and finite positive `buyavgprice` are adopted as MANUAL_PROTECTED. Product is persisted, restored and passed through `angel_orders.product_for` to the existing execution ledger's SELL submission. Existing positions without a product retain their previous product inference.

New risk settings next to the existing settings: MANUAL_PROTECT_ENABLED=True, MANUAL_HARD_STOP_PCT=0.30, MANUAL_TRAIL_ACTIVATE_PCT=0.10. At entry 100, the stop is 70. Once the observed peak reaches +10%, the existing `trade_policy.ratchet` retains 50% of peak gain. Manual stops/trails bypass the own-trade exit branch throughout 15:15–15:40. Carryforward/NRML manual positions are not closed by EOD, daily portfolio loss flattening or the owned-position watchdog; only their stop/trail or an explicit already-queued exit applies. Intraday manual positions retain EOD and daily-loss handling. Structure updates cannot overwrite manual protection with an automatic strategy stop.

External pending orders block adoption only on the same (exchange, token). An unrelated unresolved KADIMA intent continues blocking new entries, but broker-readable manual contracts can now be adopted and verified individually. Matching pending orders and unresolved quantities still prevent conflicting exits. No reconciliation guard is bypassed.

Unprotectable positive holdings queue the requested warning once per observed holding in the running engine; deduplication resets on engine restart. Missing live exit quotes queue a warning once per signal in that engine process. Opting out with MANUAL_PROTECT_ENABLED=False or the existing adopt_external_positions=False disables new manual adoption. The current Celestial rollout/master and readable broker books are still required for adoption.

`/api/kadima/top20` reports source MANUAL_PROTECTED, product, MANUAL strategy, and effective stop. A `manual_position_adopted` audit event records the accepted basis and stop for future diagnosis.

These are **engine-monitored SELL limits, not stop orders hosted at Angel One**. They require a running engine, broker-verified quantity and a fresh quote; a submission acknowledgement is not a fill or a guaranteed maximum loss.
